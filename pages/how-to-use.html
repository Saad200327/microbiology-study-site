📖 How to Use This Site

This site converts the full OpenStax Microbiology textbook (1,000+ pages) into a fast, interactive, student-friendly website. Here’s how to get the most out of it.

📐 Page Layout Explained

Every chapter page has the same layout so you always know where things are:

  1. Lesson Overview — Big picture: what this chapter is about and why it matters.
  2. Core Concepts — The main content, broken into short sections. Definitions in blue boxes. Warnings in red. Tips in green.
  3. Deeper Dive — Click the arrow to expand extra detail. Skip on first read.
  4. Real-World Examples — Orange boxes showing clinical or everyday connections.
  5. Common Exam Traps — Red warning boxes for things students frequently get wrong.
  6. Mnemonics & Memory Hooks — Yellow boxes with memorization tricks.
  7. TL;DR & Key Takeaways — Dark green box at the bottom. Start here if you’re pressed for time.
  8. Practice — MCQs and flashcards inline on each page.

💡 Pro Tips for Busy Students

  • Read the TL;DR first, then go back for details if needed.
  • Use the sidebar search (top bar) to jump directly to any term.
  • On mobile: tap the ☰ hamburger menu to navigate chapters.
  • Do at least 5 MCQs after each chapter — active recall triples retention.
  • The night before an exam: go to Exam Week Mode and read one summary per chapter.
💡 Spaced Repetition Tip
Study a chapter, then come back to the flashcards 24 hours later, then 3 days later. This is the most scientifically proven way to memorize microbiology for exams.
