🧫 Chapter 3: The Cell

🎯 Lesson Overview
All living things are made of cells. In microbiology, we focus on two types: prokaryotes (bacteria and archaea — no nucleus) and eukaryotes (fungi, protozoa, algae — have a nucleus). Understanding their structural differences explains antibiotic targets and why drugs can kill bacteria without harming human cells.

🦠 Prokaryotic Cell Structure

Prokaryotes are the simplest cells — no membrane-bound organelles, no true nucleus. Their DNA floats in a region called the nucleoid.

Key Prokaryotic Structures

🌎 Why Prokaryotic Ribosomes Matter
Prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S (30S + 50S subunits). Human ribosomes are 80S (40S + 60S). Many antibiotics (tetracyclines, aminoglycosides, erythromycin) target the 70S ribosome specifically, killing bacteria without harming human cells. This is called selective toxicity.

🧬 Eukaryotic Cell Structure

Eukaryotic cells are larger and more complex. They contain membrane-bound organelles, a true nucleus, and their ribosomes are 80S.

OrganelleFunction
NucleusHouses linear chromosomes; site of transcription
MitochondriaATP production via cellular respiration; has own 70S ribosomes (endosymbiosis!)
Endoplasmic reticulum (rough/smooth)Rough ER: protein synthesis/processing; Smooth ER: lipid synthesis, detox
Golgi apparatusModifies, packages, and ships proteins
LysosomeDigestion of waste/pathogens (animal cells)
Ribosome (80S)Protein synthesis (40S + 60S)
CytoskeletonStructure and movement (actin, tubulin)
Cell wallPresent in fungi (chitin), algae, plants (cellulose) — NOT in animal cells
🧠 Mnemonic: 70S vs 80S
"70 Pro, 80 Eu": PROkaryotes have 70S ribosomes; EUkaryotes have 80S. 70 comes before 80 just like Pro comes before Eu in evolution.

Size1–10 μm10–100 μm

⚡ TL;DR — Chapter 3 Key Takeaways

  • Prokaryotes: no nucleus, no organelles, 70S ribosomes, circular DNA, peptidoglycan cell wall
  • Eukaryotes: true nucleus, organelles, 80S ribosomes (except mitochondria), linear DNA
  • Antibiotic selectivity exploits differences (e.g., 70S ribosomes, peptidoglycan)
  • Capsule = major virulence factor (protects bacteria from phagocytosis)
  • Endospores: extreme survival structures made by Bacillus & Clostridium (resistant to heat, disinfectants)
